Forgot your password?

AuraPlayFF accounts don't use email for recovery. Password resets are handled by our admin team to keep your account secure.

1
Submit a recovery request using the button below. Provide your username, Free Fire UID, and a contact method so our team can verify your identity.
2
Our support team will verify your identity and issue you a one-time reset token (valid for 30 minutes) through your contact channel.
3
Use the token to set a new password on the reset page. The token can only be used once.
